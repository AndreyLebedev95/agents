# Requirements: Order Export Feature

1. The system shall provide a fast export of orders for the finance team.
2. The export button shall be added to the orders dashboard.
3. Exported files must be formatted so that finance can reconcile them against the accounting system with reasonable effort.
4. The export shall support all orders placed by users.
5. The export process must complete before the finance team's monthly close.
6. If an order has a refund associated with it, the refund amount shall be excluded from the exported total.
7. All orders, including refunded ones, shall be included in the export with their original amount.
